    About this experiment & device check

    FP32 streaming Parakeet encoder + Pardon turn head + Silero speech activity. Encoder inference uses WebGPU with GPU-resident recurrent caches; small head/VAD operations run locally on WebAssembly. Some encoder operations may also use CPU fallback.

    One-speaker mode is a silent-companion baseline. Its accuracy has not been established on single-speaker tasks. Two-speaker mode requires one speaker on each stereo channel; it does not separate mixed voices. Browser resampling and microphone processing can differ from the research pipeline.

    Research preview: numerical conversion checks are not a TurnBench score. No transcript, server inference, microphone upload, or benchmark submission. Model files are public and cached by your browser when storage is available.
