Turn-End Listening Test

You play the voice agent. Each clip is one caller turn from LiveKit's eot-bench. Press the moment you would start talking. Replying in a pause where the caller goes on counts as cutting them off; waiting too long costs latency.

Before you start

  1. Use wired headphones or speakers. Bluetooth adds a delay the page cannot see.
  2. Read what the agent just said, then press Enter or Play to hear the caller.
  3. Press Space or tap the big pad when you would start replying. If the caller was only pausing, they carry on talking and you can press again at a later pause. Once the caller has really stopped, a press ends the clip.
  4. If you would not reply, just wait. The clip ends 3.5 s after the caller stops.
Measures how fast you react to a sound stopping, so prediction can be told apart from reaction.
